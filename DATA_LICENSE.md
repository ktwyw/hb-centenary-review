# Data licence

## Data generated in this work

The files in `data/` created for this work (synthetic calibration ensembles, fitted-model outputs,
reconstructed curves and the JSON and CSV result records), the plotted data in `data/figure_data/`,
the table files in `data/tables/`, the figures in `figures/` and the explanatory text of the
notebooks in `notebooks/` are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The code cells of the notebooks
and all scripts are MIT-licensed (`LICENSE`).

## Third-party data

Values transcribed from the tables of published articles (Herschel & Bulkley 1926), digitized from a
published figure (Ovarlez, Krishan & Cohen-Addad 2010), computed from coefficients printed in
published articles (de Cagny et al. 2019; Magnon & Cayeux 2021; Metzner & Reed 1955; Dodge & Metzner
1959; Seth et al. 2011), compiled from the literature (`data/hb_parameter_atlas.csv`,
`data/tables/`) or retrieved from OpenAlex (`data/hb_bibliometrics.json`, CC0) remain the results of
those sources: the conditions of the original sources apply, and they should be cited when the
values are reused. `data/README.md` names the source of every file. No publisher PDF or figure
image is redistributed in this repository.
